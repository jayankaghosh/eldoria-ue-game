# Recording the Eldoria story

Thank you for voicing this. Nine short lines, about a minute and a half in total, one audio file per line.

## How to record

- **Any recorder works.** The iPhone *Voice Memos* app is perfect, as is QuickTime Player on the Mac (File > New Audio Recording).
- **A quiet room** with soft furnishings (a bedroom or a car works well). No fan, AC or TV in the background.
- **Phone about a hand-span (15-20 cm) from your mouth**, slightly to the side so your breath doesn't pop on "p" and "b".
- **Speak slower than feels natural**, like a film trailer narrator. Low, steady and deliberate. Pauses are good.
- **Do 2-3 takes** of a line if you like, and send the best one. A second or two of silence at the start and end is fine; it gets trimmed automatically.
- Read the lines **exactly as written**. The subtitles show this text. If you change any words, tell me and I'll update the subtitles to match.

## How to send them

Name the files **Intro_01** to **Intro_09** (`.m4a`, `.wav`, `.mp3` or `.aiff` are all fine) and put them in this folder:

`/Users/joy/Projects/playground/3dgame/Story/MyRecordings`

From your iPhone, AirDrop them to the Mac (they land in Downloads), then drag them into that folder. Then tell me. I'll trim and level them, put them in the game and play the story through to check the timing. Each shot automatically lasts as long as your line.

## The script

| File | Line | How to say it |
|---|---|---|
| **Intro_01** | Eldoria. A kingdom of quiet valleys and old forests, where villages slept safe behind the oath of their knights. | Slow and calm, like opening an old book. Pause after "Eldoria." |
| **Intro_02** | For three hundred years, the Royal Key kept that peace. Forged by the first kings, it sealed away every dark thing that hungered for this land. | Warm, a little proud. This is the peace that was. |
| **Intro_03** | But Mordrak, the king's own sorcerer, feared one thing above all else. His own death. | Darker and quieter. Let "His own death." land on its own. |
| **Intro_04** | He stole the Royal Key, and in the Sky Shrine above the eastern ruins, he bound his soul to bone. He did not die. He became the Bone King. | Building menace. A real pause before "He became the Bone King." |
| **Intro_05** | His champions seized the ruins. His warband of dusk-born soldiers burned the fields. And the knights of Eldoria fell, one by one. | Heavy and sad. Slow down on "one by one." |
| **Intro_06** | Now his warband camps at the edge of the last free village, and Mordrak himself waits in its square, behind a ward that no blade can break. | Urgent but low. The threat is right on the doorstep. |
| **Intro_07** | Only one knight of the Silver Oath still stands. Sir Aldric. Exiled. Forgotten. And come home. | The turn. Quiet, then firm: "Sir Aldric." A beat, then "Exiled. Forgotten. And come home." |
| **Intro_08** | Break the warband. Cut down the champions at the ruins. Climb to the Sky Shrine and take back the Royal Key. Then end the Bone King's reign. | A call to arms. Steady and rising, each task its own beat. |
| **Intro_09** | Rise, Sir Aldric. Eldoria is waiting. | Soft first, then strong. Give "Eldoria is waiting" room to breathe. |

The story is about Mordrak, the king's sorcerer, who stole the Royal Key and became the undying Bone King, and Sir Aldric, the last knight of the Silver Oath, who comes home to free the kingdom.
